Conversations That Transform How We Lead, Work, and Live.

Meet Your Host

Nathan R. Mitchell

Leadership Strategist • Executive Advisor • Author • Founder, Lead Empowered LLC

My purpose in life is to empower people.

I created Lead Empowered TV because I wanted to build a platform that could do two things at the same time: give people with meaningful messages an opportunity to reach a wider audience, while giving others a reason to believe that hope is still possible.

Every day, we encounter stories that can leave us discouraged—stories of division, uncertainty, failure, and disappointment. But there are also people doing remarkable things. People building businesses. Writing books. Overcoming challenges. Creating change. Leading with purpose. And helping others find their way forward.

Those are the stories I want to amplify.

The Mission

Give Great Messages a Platform. Give People a Reason to Hope.

Lead Empowered TV is a place where meaningful stories, practical insights, and authentic conversations can be heard.

Through conversations with business owners, entrepreneurs, authors, thought leaders, influencers, and changemakers, Lead Empowered TV explores the experiences, ideas, and insights that can help us lead stronger, work smarter, and live more empowered.

I believe everyone has a story worth sharing—and sometimes, the right conversation can change someone's perspective, restore their hope, or inspire them to take their next step.

Our Philosophy

“You have a message. Your story can make a difference. Let's give it a platform.”
