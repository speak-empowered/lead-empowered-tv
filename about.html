Meet Your Host

Nathan R. Mitchell

Leadership strategist • Executive advisor • Author • Founder, Lead Empowered LLC

Nathan believes some of the most meaningful leadership insights don't come from a textbook—they come from real conversations with people who have experienced the challenges, failures, breakthroughs, and successes that shape how we lead and live.

As host of Lead Empowered TV, Nathan creates a platform where leaders, entrepreneurs, authors, thought leaders, and changemakers can share their stories, expertise, and ideas with a growing audience.

“Your story. Your expertise. Your opportunity to be seen.”
