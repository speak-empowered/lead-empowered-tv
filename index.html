Why Streaming Television?

The way people watch television is changing.

Streaming has moved from an alternative to a central part of how audiences consume television.

Nielsen data cited by Lead Empowered TV shows streaming rising from 27% of TV viewing in May 2021 to 39.6% in August 2025, before adding 6.8% from free ad-supported television.

For brands and thought leaders, that means the opportunity to reach audiences through streaming television is increasingly relevant.

TV Viewership by Media Type

May 2021: Cable 39% · Streaming 27% · Broadcast 25% · Other 9%

August 2025: Streaming 39.6%* · FAST 6.8%* · Cable 22.5% · Broadcast 19.1% · Other 12%

*Streaming excludes FAST in the 39.6% figure. FAST = Free Ad-Supported Television. Source noted by Lead Empowered TV: Nielsen Television Research Data.
